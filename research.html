Research

Publications

My work lies in Number Theory, particularly in Diophantine equations, abelian varieties, Galois representations, and modular forms.

“I am beginning to believe that nothing can ever be proved. These are honest hypotheses which take the facts into account: but I sense so definitely that they come from me, and that they are simply a way of unifying my own knowledge [...]. Slow, lazy, sulky, the facts adapt themselves to the rigour of the order I wish to give them; but it remains outside of them. I have the feeling of doing a work of pure imagination.”

J.P. Sartre, Nausea.
